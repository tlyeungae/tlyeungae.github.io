# tlyeungae.github.io

Personal academic website of **Tai Lo Yeung**, PhD candidate in the
Department of Economics at the Università della Svizzera italiana (USI),
Lugano, Switzerland.

The site lists working papers, work in progress, teaching material,
discussant assignments, and contact information. The live version is
hosted by GitHub Pages at
[https://tlyeungae.github.io](https://tlyeungae.github.io).

## Research

Household finance, with an emphasis on how lived experience becomes
memory and shapes current financial decisions. The research studies
portfolio entry and trading, saving, retirement leakage, and housing,
alongside the measurement of beliefs and preferences.

A full list of working papers, abstracts, figures, and conference history
is available on the research page, together with downloadable current
drafts.
